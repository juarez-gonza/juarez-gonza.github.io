* What is this about?


When studying [[https://www.open-std.org/jtc1/sc22/wg21/docs/papers/2024/p2300r10.html][P2300R10]] (Senders & Receivers proposal for C++26) the
similarities between the proposal and Continuation Passing Style (CPS)
seemed apparent from the beginning.  Attempting to write down a
mapping between CPS terminology to S&R concepts was, however, harder
than I thought. Some talks and posts out there seem to notice this
too, but I have not found any resource introducing a crystal clear
mapping of what senders, receivers, operation states, schedulers,
etc. would be in the context of CPS. This post is my attempt to
produce such a mapping.
