What is this about?

When studying P2300R10 (Senders & Receivers proposal for C++26) the similarities between the proposal and Continuation Passing Style (CPS) seemed apparent from the beginning. Attempting to write down a mapping between CPS terminology to S&R concepts was, however, harder than I thought. Some talks and posts out there seem to notice this too, but I have not found any resource introducing a crystal clear mapping of what senders, receivers, operation states, schedulers, etc. would be in the context of CPS. This post is my attempt to produce such a mapping.
